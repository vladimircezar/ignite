# Cross-shaped t explorations

The original layout and both matchstick-i variants use a single charcoal (#333333) cross silhouette in place of the t. The t itself is the cross: there is no inset, outline, or frame. Its horizontal arms extend equally from the stem, and the stem ends straight at the baseline. The dark charcoal takes its cue from the supplied tonal reference.

Each logo is provided as a self-contained SVG and transparent PNG. `ignite-logo-cross-comparison.png` shows all three together. The previous no-cross comparison, approved brand assets, website, and presentation slides remain unchanged.
