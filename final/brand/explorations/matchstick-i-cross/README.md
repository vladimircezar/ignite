# Cross-shaped t explorations

Revised to follow the supplied hand-drawn reference. Areas marked red in the sketch retain the original black (#000000); the dark cross area becomes light brown (#B9926A).

The t retains its curved black foot. Its upper stem and balanced horizontal arms form the light-brown cross, which reaches the outer edges of the letter without a black inset frame.

The original layout and both matchstick-i alternatives are included as self-contained SVGs and transparent PNGs. `ignite-logo-cross-comparison.png` shows all three. Approved brand assets, website, slides, and the no-cross comparison are unchanged.
