# Matchstick i explorations

Review-only alternatives to the current Ignite in Him logo.

- `ignite-matchstick-first-i.svg` / `.png`: replaces the first i in ignite.
- `ignite-matchstick-second-i.svg` / `.png`: replaces the second i in ignite.
- `ignite-logo-comparison.png`: current logo and both alternatives on a neutral background.

The alternatives reuse the approved vector letterforms, matchstick paths, blue (#315DF2), and yellow (#FDC904). The matchstick is scaled uniformly, aligned to the word baseline, and given extra horizontal space. The “in Him” tagline is outlined from the bundled Poppins Medium font. SVGs are self-contained; individual PNGs have transparent backgrounds.

These are additional review assets only. Existing brand files, website, and presentation decks are unchanged.
